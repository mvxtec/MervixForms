# Jotform Competitors: Online Form Builder Market Comparison (research as of 2026-11-03)

Pricing note: Vendor pricing pages fetched on 2026-11-03 are marked "(fetched 2026-11-03)". Third-party figures carry the source's date where one was given. Third-party sources often disagree with each other because some quote monthly billing and some quote annual billing, so prefer the vendor-page figures.

## Q1. Positioning, target customer, entry price, free tier, standout features, and gaps vs Jotform for each competitor

### Takeaway
Jotform is the broadest self-serve "all-in-one" suite. It covers forms, payments, e-sign, PDFs, approvals, HIPAA on Gold, and now AI Agents with voice and SMS. Every plan is single-user except Enterprise. Most competitors beat it on one thing:
- Free-tier generosity: Tally, Fillout, Google Forms.
- UX and design: Typeform, Tally.
- Seat pricing: Fillout has unlimited seats; Cognito and Zoho bundle multiple users.
- Ecosystem fit: Google, Microsoft, Zoho, WordPress plugins.
- Enterprise document workflows: Formstack.

### Inferences
- **Jotform's main structural weakness is seats.** All self-serve plans are single-user. Fillout (unlimited seats), Tally Pro (unlimited team members), Cognito Team (5 users), and Zoho Standard (10 users) are cheaper for teams.
- **Jotform's main structural strength is HIPAA at Gold** ($99/mo annual, single-user). Competitors generally put HIPAA higher or don't offer it:
  - Typeform: Enterprise only.
  - Cognito: Enterprise only.
  - Zoho: Premium only.
  - Formstack: paid add-on.
  - Paperform, Tally, Fillout: none advertised.
- **On submissions per dollar, Jotform is mid-to-weak.** Zoho Basic gives 10,000 submissions per month at about $10. Fillout Business gives unlimited at $60 annual. Paperform Pro gives unlimited under fair use at $49. Jotform Gold gives 10,000 at $99.
- **Jotform's AI Agents, metered on every plan (voice minutes, SMS, knowledge base), have no clear equivalent among form-builder competitors.** Typeform has AI follow-ups and insights, Zoho has AI credits, and Paperform has AI workflow steps.

### Gaps
- Official Wufoo, SurveyMonkey, Zoho (dollar figures), Microsoft Forms, and Google Forms pricing and limit pages were not fetched directly.
- Heyflow pricing and positioning were not found. From prior knowledge only, and unverified: it is a German funnel and lead-gen form builder for marketers.
- Cognito's calculation and repeating-section features were not confirmed from a fetched Cognito feature page.

## Q4. Where Jotform is positioned, and where competitors beat it

### Takeaway
Jotform is the breadth leader for SMB and regulated self-serve buyers: forms, payments, signatures, PDFs, HIPAA, AI agents, and a very large template library. It is beaten in four places:
- **Price and free tier:** Tally, Fillout, Google Forms, Zoho.
- **Multi-seat teams:** Fillout, Tally, Zoho, Cognito.
- **Form UX and design polish:** Typeform, Tally.
- **Enterprise document and Salesforce workflows:** Formstack.

### Cited Findings
- Jotform's free tier (5 forms, 100 submissions) is far below:
  - Tally (unlimited under fair use);
  - Fillout (1,000 per month, unlimited seats);
  - Zoho (500 per month).

  — [Jotform pricing](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/); [Tally pricing](https://tally.so/pricing); [Fillout pricing](https://www.fillout.com/pricing); [Zoho Forms pricing](https://www.zoho.com/forms/pricing.html)
- Jotform's free tier is better than those of:
  - Typeform (about 10 responses);
  - Paperform (30 submissions);
  - Cognito (100 entries, roughly equal);
  - Formstack (no free plan).

  — [SaaSSwitcher](https://www.saasswitcher.com/blog/jotform-alternatives); [Paperform pricing](https://paperform.com/pricing/); [Cognito pricing](https://www.cognitoforms.com/pricing); [Formstack pricing](https://www.formstack.com/pricing)
- Multi-user is Enterprise-only at Jotform. — [Jotform pricing](https://www.jotform.com/pricing/)
- Higher-volume Typeform plans cost more than Jotform's. — [SaaSSwitcher](https://www.saasswitcher.com/blog/jotform-alternatives)

### Inferences
- **Jotform wins on:** HIPAA at a mid-tier price, all-in-one breadth, template volume, and AI Agents bundled into every plan.
- **Jotform is most at risk from:**
  - Tally and Fillout among startups, no-code users, and Airtable/Notion users (free tier, seats, modern UX);
  - Google and Microsoft Forms inside existing suites, for zero-cost basics;
  - Zoho for teams that need volume at low cost.

  Formstack and Cognito compete for regulated and ops-heavy buyers, but both gate HIPAA or approvals at higher prices.

### Gaps
- No G2 or Capterra rating comparison was fetched directly. Satisfaction claims rely on a G2 compare snippet only.
