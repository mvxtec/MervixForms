# Jotform Product Suite Beyond the Form Builder

Research date: 2026-11-03. Sources are mainly Jotform's own pages (product pages, help center, blog/announcements), Jotform's Product Hunt launch list, and press-release syndications. Some web-fetch results are summaries of pages, so check exact wording against the source before quoting.

## Q1. What does each Jotform product do, what are its key features, how does it connect to forms, and when did it launch?

### Takeaway
Jotform grew from a form builder (2006) into a suite of about 15 or more products. Nearly every product either consumes form submissions (Tables, Reports, Inbox, Boards, PDF), drives a submission (Apps, Store Builder, AI Agents, Sign, Workflows), or administers them (Teams, Enterprise, HIPAA). It launched roughly one major product a year from 2018 to 2024. Since 2025 the focus has shifted heavily to AI Agents and to AI and LLM-platform apps.

- 2022: White-Label app, Store Builder, Sign (e-signatures), and Teams. — [Jotform About](https://www.jotform.com/about/); Sign's 2022 date is corroborated by [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jotform)

**Jotform Tables (launched Oct 27, 2020)**
- Announced as "a no-code, spreadsheet-powered database platform for teams" to collect, organize, and manage data in an all-in-one workspace. — [PR Newswire, Oct 27 2020](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/jotform-announces-tables-a-no-code-spreadsheet-powered-database-platform-for-teams-301160527.html)
- Views: Table, Cards, Report, Calendar, Uploads, and Online Form. Supports formulas and calculations, CSV or Excel import, manual entry, search and filter, and one-click sharing and collaboration. Action Buttons can send data to other software, create PDFs, and send emails. Templates include Sales CRM, Recruitment Tracker, and Office Inventory. Connected forms feed submissions into tables automatically. — [Jotform Tables product page](https://www.jotform.com/products/tables/)
- Jotform AI Data Assistant (Aug 2026) works on top of Tables. It can create tables, organize submissions, add columns, summarize data, generate charts, and take actions from prompts. — [Jotform Tables](https://www.jotform.com/products/tables/); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/jotform/launches)
- Tables is also the tracking layer for Workflows: it shows "who took action, where the workflow stands." — [Workflows data management](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/features/data-management/)
- A later blog post announced more team-productivity features for Tables. — [Jotform blog](https://www.jotform.com/blog/announcing-new-tables-features-for-team-productivity/)

**Jotform Reports / Report Builder (2020) and PDF Editor (2018) / Smart PDF Forms (2020)**
- Report Builder and Smart PDF Forms launched in 2020. PDF Editor launched in 2018. — [Jotform About](https://www.jotform.com/about/)
- In Workspace, Reports are described as "visual reports to analyze and share collected data." Report is also a view inside Tables. — [What is Jotform Workspace](https://www.jotform.com/help/what-is-jotform-workspace/); [Tables](https://www.jotform.com/products/tables/)
- Sign's product page directs users to Tables and Report Builder to organize and report on signed-document data. — [Jotform Sign](https://www.jotform.com/products/sign/)

**Jotform Apps (launched Nov 16, 2021) and AI App Builder (Jun 2026)**
- Announced as "a no-code app builder." — [PR Newswire, Nov 16 2021](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/jotform-apps-launches-as-a-no-code-app-builder-301424482.html)
- Apps bundle forms, widgets, products, and links. Users add them to their home screen as a web app (PWA-style), with no app-store publishing. They are shared by link, email, QR code, or social media. Access can be public, private, or company-only. Push notifications are supported; iOS users must add the app to the home screen first. Apps are included in every plan, including the free Starter plan. — [Jotform Apps](https://www.jotform.com/products/apps/)
- AI App Builder generates apps from a prompt. The App Builder is also available inside ChatGPT and Claude, where users describe, refine, and publish an app. — [Jotform Apps](https://www.jotform.com/products/apps/); [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/jotform/launches)

**Jotform Store Builder (2022)**
- Launched in 2022. — [Jotform About](https://www.jotform.com/about/)
- Store Builder is a separate online-store builder that works alongside Apps. It handles products for sale. — [Jotform Apps](https://www.jotform.com/products/apps/)

**Jotform Sign (2022)**
- Features: 1,000+ templates (agreements, waivers, consent forms); reusable documents with signer roles; PDF conversion; sending by email, link, or embed; inclusion in approval flows; automatic delivery to cloud storage; and Sign Inbox with status, reminders, and downloads. — [Jotform Sign](https://www.jotform.com/products/sign/)
- Pricing on the Sign page: Starter is free for 10 signed documents a month. Bronze is $39/mo for 100, Silver $49/mo for 250, Gold $129/mo for 1,000, and Enterprise is custom. — [Jotform Sign](https://www.jotform.com/products/sign/)
- Legal validity: the FAQ cites the US E-Sign Act (2000) and links to a guide for other countries. It notes that some documents, such as wills, need a wet signature. — [Sign FAQ](https://www.jotform.com/products/sign/faq/)
- Audit trail: records when a document was opened and signed, and possibly the signer's geolocation. The Sign Builder supports multiple signers and signing order. iOS and Android apps include an in-person signing option. — [Sign FAQ](https://www.jotform.com/products/sign/faq/)
- Sign for ChatGPT and Claude launched Sep 29, 2026. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/jotform/launches)

**Jotform Approvals (Apr 2021) → Jotform Workflows (Oct 22, 2024)**
- Approvals first launched in April 2021 and was renamed Workflows on Oct 22, 2024. — [Announcing Jotform Workflows](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/announcing-jotform-workflows)
- Elements: Form, Email, Conditional Branch (routes on form-field values), Merge Branches, Integration (22 integrations, run in sequence or in parallel), Payment Form, Task, and Approval. Approval supports multiple or group approvers and custom notifications. Tasks are assigned by email, with custom outcomes, expiration dates, and reminders. The canvas offers vertical and horizontal layouts. — [Announcing Jotform Workflows](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/announcing-jotform-workflows)
- Pricing: almost all features are available without a paid plan. White labeling is Enterprise-only. — [Announcing Jotform Workflows](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/announcing-jotform-workflows)
- Admins can restart or cancel workflows. Flow tracking shows the status of each run. — [Restart/cancel](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/features/restart-cancel-options/); [Flow tracking](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/features/flow-tracking)

**Jotform Inbox**
- Inbox shows a threaded view of each workflow run (who did what and when). Users can approve or act directly from Inbox, and admins can restart or cancel workflows there. — [Workflows: Jotform Inbox](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/features/jotform-inbox)

**Jotform Boards (announced Mar 25, 2025)**
- Kanban-style task boards with drag-and-drop. Tasks are created automatically from form submissions, AI Agent conversations (chat, calls, and other channels), Workflows triggers, payment-form orders, and signed e-sign documents. — [MarTech Series, Mar 25 2025](https://martechseries.com/?p=375094)
- Tasks support assignees, priorities, due dates, tags, checklists, files, links, comments, and an activity log. Boards include custom fields, themes, access-controlled sharing, and 1,000+ templates. Boards come at no extra cost for Jotform account holders. — [Boards workflow-task connection](https://www.jotform.com/products/boards/features/workflow-task-connection); [finanzen.net PR](https://finanzen.net/nachricht/aktien/jotform-boards-launches-as-a-powerful-new-way-to-capture-and-resolve-every-customer-request-14342257)

**Jotform AI Agents (2025) and the AI Chatbot**
- Listed as a 2025 launch that "automate[s] tasks across 15 channels." — [Jotform About](https://www.jotform.com/about/)
- The features page now lists 16 channels: Standalone, Chatbot, Phone, Voice, WhatsApp, Messenger, SMS, Agent App, Kiosk, Shopify, Presentation, Gmail, Instagram, WordPress chatbot, Salesforce, and Canva chatbot. It also claims 7,000+ agent templates and 35 tools. — [AI Agents features](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/features/)
- Training has five parts: AI Persona (name, role, about 20 languages, tone, response length); a Knowledge Base built from text entries, website URLs (subpages crawled), uploaded files, and Q&A pairs; Actions (conditional "if X then Y" rules, such as API requests, triggering workflows, or sending emails); Tools (Google Calendar booking, OneDrive, Slack, and "Take Note"); and Teach Your Agent (training by chat). — [Announcing Jotform AI Agents](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/announcing-jotform-ai-agents/)
- Forms connect to agents in two ways. An agent can be linked to one or more forms and fill the fields through conversation. Any form can also be turned into an agent from the Publish or Quick Share panels. — [Announcing Jotform AI Agents](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/announcing-jotform-ai-agents/)
- The Conversations view holds searchable and downloadable transcripts, with thumbs-up/down feedback. A human can take over in the mobile app. — [Announcing Jotform AI Agents](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/announcing-jotform-ai-agents/)
- Pricing and model: agents are free within plan limits on agent, conversation, and session counts. Phone and SMS numbers cost $10 per number per month. The page names OpenAI GPT-4 as the model, says customer data is not used for training, and says OpenAI may retain data for 30 days. — [Announcing Jotform AI Agents](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/announcing-jotform-ai-agents/)
- On Enterprise servers, agents and their phone, SMS, and voice channels are off by default and are enabled in the Admin Console. — [AI Agents for Jotform Enterprise](https://www.jotform.com/blog/announcing-ai-agents-for-jotform-enterprise)

### Inferences
- Most products are bundled into the same plan tiers rather than sold separately. Apps, Workflows, Boards, and Agents are "free within plan limits." Sign has its own document-count tiers on its page. A competitor faces a "free everything, metered by quota" pricing model.
- Products are generally layered on top of the form engine rather than built as separate systems. For example, Agents fill form fields, Apps embed forms, and Store Builder uses payment forms.

- The exact launch date of the Inbox and the first AI Agents announcement was not found. The About page says only "2025."
- On-prem hosting: no evidence found. Only a dedicated cloud instance is described.
- Signing compliance beyond ESIGN (UETA, eIDAS) and bulk send are not documented on the pages fetched.
- Store Builder has no 2026 feature page reviewed.

## Q2. How do these products share data, and what is the platform architecture from the user's view?

### Takeaway
The form submission is the central record. Tables stores submissions, and Reports, Inbox, Boards, and PDFs read from them. Agents, Apps, Store Builder, Sign, and Workflows produce or route submissions. All of this appears in a single "My Workspace" hub with a Create menu by asset type, folders and labels, and Team Workspaces.

### Cited Findings
- My Workspace is a single hub for Forms, AI Agents, Apps, E-sign Documents, Workflows, Tables, and Reports. It replaced My Forms as the account homepage, though My Forms is still reachable from a dropdown. — [What is Jotform Workspace](https://www.jotform.com/help/what-is-jotform-workspace/); [Welcome to the new My Workspace](https://www.jotform.com/blog/welcome-to-the-new-my-workspace/)
- The Create button lists asset types: Form, Table, Report, App, Sign Document, Workflow, and AI Agent. The sidebar has Shared with Me, Assigned to Me (forms and workflows), Sent, and Drafts. — [What is Jotform Workspace](https://www.jotform.com/help/what-is-jotform-workspace/)
- Labels, a "supercharged version of folders," apply across forms, reports, tables, Sign documents, apps, workflows, boards, and AI Agents. Enterprise users also see Team Workspaces. — [Announcing labels](https://www.jotform.com/blog/announcing-labels-across-your-jotform-enterprise-workspace); [Enterprise Team Workspace](https://www.jotform.com/enterprise/features/team-workspace/)
- Forms feed Tables automatically, Tables tracks workflow runs, and Boards turns submissions, agent conversations, workflow triggers, orders, and signed documents into tasks. — [Tables](https://www.jotform.com/products/tables/); [Workflows data management](https://www.jotform.com/products/workflows/features/data-management/); [MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/?p=375094)
- AI Agents fill linked forms through conversation, and conversations sit in their own Conversations store. — [Announcing Jotform AI Agents](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/announcing-jotform-ai-agents/)

### Inferences
- The architecture looks like a hub and spokes: the form/submission schema is the hub, and every product is a different input channel (agent, app, store, sign, mobile) or output view (table, report, board, inbox, PDF) over it. For a competitor, the most important design decision is a single canonical submission record shared by all modules.
- Sign documents and Agent conversations appear to be secondary record types next to submissions. Both feed Boards and Tables.

### Gaps
- Public material does not show the internal data model, for example whether Sign documents are stored as form submissions internally.

## Q3. Which products are newest, and what is Jotform's strategic direction?

### Takeaway
Since 2025 nearly every launch has been AI-centered: AI Agents (16 channels), Presentation Agents, AI form building, the AI App Builder, and the AI Data Assistant. Jotform is also distributing its products inside other platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Canva, Salesforce, Shopify, WordPress, and Gmail. The strategy is "AI agents and AI creation everywhere," with forms as the data back-end.

### Cited Findings
- The ten most recent Product Hunt launches (Nov 2025 to Oct 2026) are all AI products, LLM-platform apps, or new embeddable surfaces. They are Enterprise 2.0, AI Chatbot for Canva, Jotform AI, the ChatGPT app, the Claude app, AI App Builder, Website Widgets, AI Data Assistant, Sign for ChatGPT/Claude, and App Builder for ChatGPT & Claude. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/jotform/launches)
- The About page highlights AI Agents as the 2025 milestone. The agents features page now lists 16 channels and 7,000+ agent templates. — [Jotform About](https://www.jotform.com/about/); [AI Agents features](https://www.jotform.com/ai/agents/features/)
- The Enterprise page markets AI Agents for round-the-clock customer service and an AI Brand Kit. — [Jotform Enterprise](https://www.jotform.com/enterprise/)
- Boards (Mar 2025) was positioned to "capture and resolve every customer request," which moves Jotform toward help-desk and ticketing. — [MarTech Series](https://martechseries.com/?p=375094)

### Inferences
- Jotform is moving from "form builder" to "no-code customer-interaction and operations platform," with agents as the front door and Boards and Workflows as the back office. This puts it closer to Intercom/Zendesk-style tools and to Airtable, Monday, and DocuSign in addition to Typeform.
- Building apps inside ChatGPT and Claude suggests Jotform expects LLM assistants to become a primary interface for creating forms and apps.

### Gaps
- No official Jotform strategy statement or CEO interview was reviewed. The direction is inferred from the pattern of launches.
- Revenue or adoption figures per product (for example, how many AI Agents are in use) were not found.
